Data Sources & Open Portals

Satellite Missions & Observational Archives

Every environmental layer, coordinate lookup, and time series inside AETHRA is grounded in publicly accessible NASA satellite datasets and APIs.

NASA Earthdata Portal

Primary gateway to Earth science satellite collections, multi sensor archives, and geospatial tools.

https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/
↗

NASA Worldview & GIBS

Global Imagery Browse Services providing daily satellite raster tiles for surface temperature, true color reflectance, and NDVI.

https://worldview.earthdata.nasa.gov/
↗

NASA POWER Project API

Prediction Of Worldwide Energy Resources API supplying live daily solar irradiance, surface temperature, humidity, and precipitation series for any clicked map point.

https://power.larc.nasa.gov/
↗

NASA FIRMS (MODIS & VIIRS)

Fire Information for Resource Management System tracking near real time active fire hotspots and thermal anomalies.

https://firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/
↗

NASA SMAP Soil Moisture

Soil Moisture Active Passive observatory measuring surface and root zone soil water content for drought monitoring.

https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/data/platforms/space-based-platforms/smap
↗

NASA GRACE & GRACE FO

Gravity Recovery and Climate Experiment Follow On mission tracking monthly terrestrial water storage and ice mass changes.

https://gracefo.jpl.nasa.gov/data/grace-fo-data/
↗

NASA / USGS Landsat Program

High resolution multispectral land surface reflectance, urban heat island, and canopy change observations.

https://science.nasa.gov/mission/landsat/
↗

NASA ISRO NISAR & ASF DAAC

Dual frequency Synthetic Aperture Radar measurements for millimeter scale ground subsidence and cryospheric deformation.

https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/data/platforms/space-based-platforms/nisar
↗
