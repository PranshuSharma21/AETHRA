AETHRA • Earth Observation Intelligence

Turning satellite data into clear answers about our planet.

Satellites collect massive amounts of information about heat, rainfall, soil moisture, and underground water every single day. AETHRA brings those separate measurements together into one simple map so anyone can click a location, see what is changing right now, and understand what could happen over the next thirty days.

How AETHRA Works

Simple on the surface, rigorous underneath

Instead of overwhelming you with raw satellite files, AETHRA processes five layers of environmental readings automatically and keeps the advanced scientific sliders tucked away until you want them.

01

Pick Any Point on Earth

Click anywhere on the interactive satellite map or choose one of our tracked global regions. AETHRA immediately pulls daily temperature, rainfall, and humidity observations from NASA satellites for that exact coordinate.

02

Separate Facts From Forecasts

We clearly separate three things that are often confused: how unusual today's weather is compared to the past thirty years, how high the combined environmental risk is from zero to one hundred, and how temperatures are trending over the next thirty days.

03

Explore Scenarios & Export

Open the optional advanced drawer to test how extra heat or lower rainfall would shift regional risk, ask the AI assistant questions across all stations, and export a visual snapshot card, spreadsheet, or text briefing.

What AETHRA Measures

Five interconnected signals from space

Looking at temperature alone rarely tells the full story. Droughts, crop stress, and wildfires happen when several factors line up at the same time:

Land Surface Temperature MODIS & NASA POWER
Root Zone Soil Moisture NASA SMAP Mission
Underground Water Storage GRACE Follow On
Vegetation Health & Canopy MODIS Terra NDVI
Active Thermal & Fire Detections NASA FIRMS VIIRS
