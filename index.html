04 • SCIENTIFIC CLARITY & ARCHITECTURE

Why We Separate Three Distinct Numbers

Many platforms mix current weather anomalies with future predictions. AETHRA keeps them distinct so you always know what is observed today versus what is projected ahead.

METRIC 01 Isolation Forest

Observed Anomaly (σ)

Measures how far today's satellite readings sit from the thirty year historical average for that exact location. A reading above two sigma means conditions are statistically unusual right now.

METRIC 02 Logistic Fusion

Composite Risk (0 to 100)

Evaluates how multiple stresses combine. High heat paired with wet soil is manageable, but high heat paired with depleted soil moisture and groundwater creates severe compound risk.

METRIC 03 Holt Smoothing

Thirty Day Forecast (°C)

Projects where surface temperatures are heading over the next thirty days using double exponential smoothing across recent daily NASA POWER observations.

05 • QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

All observations come from public NASA portals, including live daily meteorological queries to the NASA POWER API, satellite raster overlays from NASA Worldview and GIBS, soil moisture from NASA SMAP, water storage from GRACE Follow On, and active fire detections from NASA FIRMS.

You can click any location anywhere on Earth. AETHRA drops a live marker at that exact latitude and longitude, fetches the past thirty days of daily observations from the NASA POWER API, and computes the local anomaly, risk score, and thirty day forecast on the fly.

Every time you ask a question, the dashboard injects the live numerical readings from all monitored stations directly into the system prompt sent to our Groq and Qwen backend (with a built in analytical ranking engine as an instant fallback), ensuring every answer cites verified station numbers.
